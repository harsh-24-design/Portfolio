HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTOGRAPH
====================================

1. Place your photo in this folder:
   c:\Users\Deepak\OneDrive\Desktop\mera\portfolio\assets\images\profile.jpg
   (or .png / .webp)

2. In index.html, find the <img> tag in the hero section:
   <img src="assets/images/avatar-placeholder.svg" alt="Harsh Raghuwanshi" id="hero-profile-img">

3. Update the src attribute to:
   <img src="assets/images/profile.jpg" alt="Harsh Raghuwanshi" id="hero-profile-img">

That's it! Your photo will automatically inherit the circular styling, subtle glowing ring, and responsive sizing.
